# Falcon 9 · Flight Control

Interactive HTML, CSS and JavaScript flight simulator. The complete application lives in `public/flight.html`, `public/flight.css`, `public/flight.js` and `public/physics.js`. Three.js is vendored locally with its MIT license. The Sites wrapper serves it at the root.

For local testing, double-click `START-LOCAL.cmd` and open http://127.0.0.1:4173. No dependencies or account are needed. See `LOCAL-START.md`. The full Sites workflow is also available with `npm ci` and `npm run dev`. Run `node verify-physics.mjs`, `node verify-world.mjs`, `node verify-interface.mjs` and `node verify-scene.mjs` for deterministic flight validation and `npm run build` for production.

Launch starts a uniform time-compressed mission (120 seconds by default). Configure playback, sound, explanation cards and rendering quality before launching. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, switch cameras, pause/resume or reset at any time. Hidden tabs automatically pause. WebGL is required; sound effects and countdown tones are generated locally through Web Audio. There is no speech synthesis.

## Model

0.05-second fixed-step integration of two-dimensional motion in SI units. Gravity varies with altitude, atmospheric density falls exponentially, aerodynamic drag opposes velocity, mass falls according to thrust / (specific impulse × standard gravity). Stage separation removes upper-stage mass. After separation, attitude and angular velocity are integrated with a mass-dependent moment of inertia, bounded control torque, and damping. Cold-gas control continues during coasting, aerodynamic authority grows with dynamic pressure, and engine gimbal is limited to 6 degrees. Thrust direction follows the integrated attitude plus gimbal. Rotation does not reset at engine cutoff. Control gains and torque limits are educational approximations, not measured SpaceX control-system parameters. Return and entry burns precede closed-loop single-engine landing guidance.

The computed trajectory lasts about 425 seconds, with a vertical ascent until 15 seconds and at least 180 metres of clearance, followed by a smooth pitch program. The booster lands below 1 m/s with remaining propellant. Vehicle geometry approximates Falcon 9's 70 m height and 3.7 m core diameter, nine engine bells, interstage, fairing, four grid fins and four deployable legs. Launch site geometry is illustrative. Vehicle dimensions, altitude and terrain share a metre scale. Earth now uses NASA surface and elevation maps, a separate animated cloud layer, an ocean specular mask and a real-time atmospheric scattering shader. The ground uses a 6,371 km Earth radius and a floating local coordinate frame; it appears flat close to the surface and curved from high altitude. A logarithmically spaced ground mesh retains local precision. The landing pad follows the same spherical ground coordinates and its foundation penetrates the terrain and its top is 16 cm above grade, preventing depth overlap. Tower umbilical arms touch the vehicle at rest and retract before liftoff. Upper-stage motion after separation is illustrative, not an independently integrated orbital trajectory. Landing pad is placed at the predicted booster endpoint; this is vertical velocity guidance, not geographic target guidance. Engine, mass and aerodynamic parameters are approximate; this is educational and is not engineering-certified SpaceX software.

Reference: https://new.spacex.com/vehicles/falcon-9

Core physics, geometry and build checks are automated. The refactored flight has also been run in the browser through completion, with launch, touchdown and 84.74 km altitude inspected. The retired shadow-map warning found during the first inspection was corrected; subsequent runs produced no new rendering errors. Audio was muted during visual checks. Detailed requirements and limits are in REFACTOR-REQUIREMENTS.md.


Touchdown now continues through a damped spring contact phase, including visible leg compression, before mission completion. The independent 20 km/h drop test settles with about 0.385 m maximum compression. Texture provenance is in public/assets/ATTRIBUTION.txt. All textures are bundled for local use.
