SIMULATION NOTES

A flight model, explained.

This independent educational simulation uses Falcon 9 proportions (70 m tall, 3.7 m diameter) and nominal sea-level thrust of 7,607 kN. Procedural meshes represent the vehicle and an illustrative launch complex.

A fixed-step, two-dimensional model integrates thrust, propellant consumption, altitude-dependent gravity, exponential atmospheric density and drag. Landing guidance modulates a single engine. Telemetry tracks the booster after separation; the upper stage continues visually.

Mass, drag, guidance and burn schedules are approximations, not SpaceX flight software or a specific mission. The vehicle, terrain and altitude share a metre scale. Earth uses a 6,371 km radius and a moving local coordinate frame. The landing location is the predicted recovery point, not a recreation of a particular SpaceX landing site. Default playback compresses the calculated flight uniformly into two minutes, including countdown.
